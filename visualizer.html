Fischer Esterification

Carboxylic acid and alcohol condense under acid catalysis to form an ester, releasing water as a by-product.

Sample data

Mechanism summary

Protonation of the carbonyl oxygen activates the acid toward nucleophilic attack by the alcohol, forming a tetrahedral intermediate that collapses with loss of water to give the ester.

Handling note

Acetic acid and ethanol are flammable and irritant. Work in a ventilated area and avoid skin contact with concentrated reagents.
